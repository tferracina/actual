Personal Actual Budget privacy notice

This instance is operated by its owner for private, noncommercial management of their own finances. It is not a public service and does not accept other users.

Budget files and imported transactions are stored on the owner's computer. The app uses browser storage and a local synchronization server. The owner manages access, backups and deletion.

When the owner explicitly connects a bank, Enable Banking processes the authorized account information and provides it to this instance. Banks and Enable Banking have their own privacy notices and consent controls. Only accounts owned and linked by the owner are intended to be accessed.

Connection credentials are stored on the local server. Actual's optional end-to-end budget encryption does not encrypt those credentials. This instance is restricted to the local computer.

The owner can revoke bank access through the bank or Enable Banking and delete local budgets and backups. This notice describes this personal installation only.
